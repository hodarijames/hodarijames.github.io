Fellowships and Scholarships

  • NSF Graduate Research Fellowship (GRFP) mid to late October · Seniors and first two years of grad school. Three years of stipend.
  • Goldwater Scholarship campus nomination in fall · Sophomores and juniors. Ask me.
  • Simons-NSBP Scholars at Flatiron mid-January · Paid summer research. NSBP members.
  • NASA FINESST February · Grad students. Your advisor is PI, you write it.
